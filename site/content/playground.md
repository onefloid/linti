---
title: Playground
description: Paste a whole TI process, lint it, and apply auto-fixes in your browser.
---

Paste a complete process and LinTi checks it as you type. `.ti` files (plain or with `#region` sections), PA code (`#SECTION` … `#JSON_PROPERTIES`), and TM1py YAML are detected automatically. **Auto-fix** rewrites the code in its original format.

Everything runs locally in your browser via Pyodide: your code is never sent to a server. Click a finding to jump to it, or open its rule ID for an explanation.

::process-playground
::
