---
title: Configurator
description: Build a linti.yaml for your project from a use case, then fine-tune every rule.
---

Start from the use case that fits your project, then adjust run settings and rules in the form. The `linti.yaml` on the right follows every change, and you can edit the YAML directly: the form picks it up. Only settings that differ from LinTi's defaults are written, so your project automatically gets improved defaults in future releases.

Save several named configurations in this browser and switch between them. Changes stay in a local draft until you save or save as a new configuration. The browser has no account or sync: clearing its data removes your profiles, so use **Export saved profiles** for a copy. A share link carries one configuration; opening it creates an unsaved draft that you can save locally.

Download a `linti.yaml` into your project, or **try it in the playground** on a real process. From the rule reference, you can open a rule here to experiment with its settings, then return to test your draft.
