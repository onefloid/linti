---
title: Configurator
description: Build a linti.yaml for your project from a use case, then fine-tune every rule.
---

Choose a preset, adjust the rules, and watch the `linti.yaml` update. You can also edit the YAML directly; the form follows along. The add button asks for a name and starts with an empty configuration. After editing, **Save configuration** stores it directly in this browser. Use the switcher to move between configurations; **Manage configurations** offers duplicate, rename, export, and import.

You can download the YAML for your project or try your draft in the playground and rule reference. A share link opens as a draft that you can save if you like.
