---
title: Configurator
description: Build a linti.yaml for your project from a use case, then fine-tune every rule.
---

Choose a preset, adjust the rules, and watch the `linti.yaml` update. You can also edit the YAML directly; the form follows along. **Save variant** keeps a named configuration in this browser. Open **Manage configurations** when you want to duplicate, rename, delete, export, or import profiles.

You can download the YAML for your project or try your draft in the playground and rule reference. A share link opens as a draft that you can save if you like.
